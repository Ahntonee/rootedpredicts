BTTS Yes means both teams will score at least one goal each. BTTS No means at least one team will fail to score. We cover both outcomes.

What is a BTTS prediction?

A Both Teams to Score (BTTS) bet wins when both teams score at least one goal each during the match. It does not matter who wins the game — the only condition is that neither team keeps a clean sheet. BTTS Yes is the most common variant, though BTTS No has value in defensive matchups.

How We Select BTTS Tips

Attacking quality of both sides

We look at how many goals both teams have been scoring per game. If both teams average over 1.5 goals per game, BTTS Yes becomes a strong candidate.

Defensive vulnerability

Teams that concede frequently are ideal BTTS Yes candidates. We check goals conceded per game and defensive injury situations before every tip.

Head-to-head BTTS record

Some fixtures have a strong historical pattern of both teams scoring. We factor in the full head-to-head BTTS record for each matchup.

Match importance

High-stakes games sometimes produce cautious, low-scoring affairs. We account for match context — cup finals, relegation battles, and dead rubbers all affect scoring patterns.

BTTS Yes vs BTTS No

BTTS Yes tips are best for attacking matchups between two teams with porous defences. BTTS No tips are best for matches involving strong defensive teams or games where one side is likely to dominate completely. What is a double chance bet?

A double chance bet covers two of the three standard outcomes in a football match — home win or draw (1X), away win or draw (X2), or home win or away win (12). Because you are covering two outcomes instead of one, double chance bets are significantly safer than 1X2 bets, though the odds are lower.

Types of Double Chance Tips We Provide

  1. 1X — Home win or draw (protects against upset losses when backing strong home sides)
  2. X2 — Away win or draw (ideal for strong away teams that could draw or win)
  3. 12 — Home win or away win (no draw — for attacking games likely to produce a definitive result)

How We Select Double Chance Tips

Team quality gap analysis

When there is a clear quality gap between two teams, 1X or X2 double chance bets offer good value. We identify these mismatches early and present them as double chance picks.

Avoiding draws in attacking games

When two attacking teams meet, the 12 double chance — home win or away win — eliminates the draw risk while still giving you a solid payout.
