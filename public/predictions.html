What is an accumulator bet?

An accumulator — also called an acca — is a single bet that links four or more individual selections together. For the bet to win, every single selection must be correct. The odds of each selection multiply together, which is why accas offer the highest potential payouts in football betting. A £10 five-fold acca at average odds of 2.0 per leg returns £320 if all five selections win.
