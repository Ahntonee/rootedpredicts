How Our Algorithm Works

show_chart
Team Form — 35%

We analyse each team's last 5 matches — wins, losses, goals scored and conceded — to calculate a form weight. Home and away performance are tracked separately.

compare_arrows
Head-to-Head — 25%

The last 10 meetings between the two sides are weighted, giving greater importance to more recent encounters and home-ground advantage.

sports_soccer
Goal Averages — 20%

Home and away goal scoring and conceding averages across the season feed into market-specific models — especially for over/under and BTTS markets.

format_list_numbered
Table Position — 20%

The gap in league standings between the two sides reflects relative quality and motivation. Top-half vs bottom-half matchups carry higher predictive weight.
