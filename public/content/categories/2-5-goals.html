No bet is guaranteed. Three goals is a higher target than two, and missed chances, a red card, injuries or a defensive game plan can change the outcome. Treat our selections as research and manage your stake responsibly.

Over 1.5 generally has a higher chance of landing because it needs fewer goals, while Over 2.5 commonly offers higher odds. The right market depends on the specific match, price and your risk tolerance.
