What is an away win prediction?

An away win prediction — also known as a "2" in 1X2 betting — wins when the visiting team wins the match. It is one of the three outcomes in a standard 1X2 market alongside the home win and the draw. Away wins are less common than home wins but they happen very regularly, especially when the away side is significantly stronger than the home team. This is what makes them attractive — away win odds are usually higher, giving you better returns.
