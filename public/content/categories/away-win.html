What does away win mean? It means the visiting team must win the football match. In a standard 1X2 market, the away win is usually represented by the number 2.

Can a draw win an away team bet? No. A standard away win bet loses if the match ends in a draw.

What is an away team to win either half? It means the away team only needs to win one of the two halves, not the full match.

What does away team to win to nil mean? It means the away team must win the match, and the home team must not score at all.

Can I find free away win predictions on Rootedpredict? Yes, we provide free away win predictions whenever the fixtures and our analysis point to a good chance.

What should I check before backing an away team? Check the away side's form, the home team's record, goals scored and conceded, suspensions, injuries, expected lineups, motivation, recent fixtures, and the current market odds.

Is a short away win price a guaranteed winner? No. Short odds like 1.40 mean the bookmakers see it as the likely outcome, but likely is not the same as certain.

Is an away win safer than an away or draw bet? No. An away win only pays out if the away team wins outright, while an away or draw bet covers both outcomes. That's why away or draw odds are usually shorter than straight away win odds.
