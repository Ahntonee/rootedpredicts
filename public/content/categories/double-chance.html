What is a double chance prediction?

A double chance bet covers two of the three standard outcomes in a football match. There are three double chance options: home win or draw (1X), away win or draw (X2), and home win or away win (12). Because you are covering two outcomes instead of one, double chance bets are significantly safer than straight 1X2 bets, though the odds are somewhat lower. This makes them ideal for bettors who want security without sacrificing all returns.
