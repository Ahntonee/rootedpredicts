A double chance bet covers two possible match outcomes in a single selection, instead of picking just one result like you would with a normal home win, draw, or away win bet.

Here are the three double chance options:

For example, say Arsenal are playing Chelsea:

If you bet 1X, you're saying Arsenal won't lose. Your bet wins if Arsenal win or draw, and only loses if Chelsea win.

If you bet X2, you're saying Chelsea won't lose.

If you bet 12, you're saying there will be a winner, without picking which team. Your bet loses only if the match ends in a draw.

This is what makes double chance different from a standard 1X2 single-outcome bet.

Here are a few things worth keeping in mind to improve your Double Chance prediction strategy

1. Don't back every favourite. If a team is known for its success, but not on a consistent basis, then it is not a 1X. Analyze the real numbers before making a choice.

2. Check home and away form separately. Some teams perform far better at home than away, and some do the opposite. This distinction can help you decide between 1X and X2.

3. Check for missing players. A team can be severely affected by the loss of its leading player in the centre-back, striker or goalkeeper.

4. Don't rule out the draw. This is important when it comes to 1X and X2 bets. If the sides are even, then there is a distinct possibility of a draw.

5. Compare the odds. The safest-looking option is not always the best value. The odds of double chance markets, naturally, are lower, as you are taking a chance on two things, rather than one.

6. Avoid stacking huge accumulators. While 10, 15 or more may seem like a great offer, a few bad bets can ruin a string of good ones. In general, it's more advantageous to go for a smaller number of choices you've been able to correctly analyze.

What is double chance? Double chance means picking two of the three possible outcomes in a football match, home win, draw, or away win, combined into a single bet.

What does 1X mean? 1X: Home team wins or draws.

What does X2 mean? X2 indicates that the away side will win or draw the game.

What does 12 mean? Either team will win if 12 is the number. In case of a draw, the bet loses.

Is double chance more likely to win? It has a better chance of winning than playing a single mark, as you're wagering on two possibilities, so the odds will be lower.
