What is a BTTS prediction?

A Both Teams to Score prediction wins when both teams score at least one goal each during the match. It does not matter who wins the game or what the final score is — the only condition is that neither team keeps a clean sheet. BTTS Yes is the most popular variant but BTTS No also has great value in defensive matchups between tight, organised teams.
