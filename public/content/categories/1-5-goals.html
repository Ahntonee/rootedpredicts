What Does Over 1.5 Goals Mean?

It means you are forecasting that the match will have a minimum of 2 goals overall.

There's one thing you have to notice about the 1.5 goals market. You do not require your team to win, nor do both teams need to score.

All you need is 2 or more goals throughout the match.

  • If the game ends 2-0, you win the bet
  • If the game ends 2-0, you win the bet.
  • Your game finishes 3-0? You win.
  • If it finishes 2-1? You win too.

Only 0-0, 1-0 and 0-1 full-time results will lose the bet with this market.

Due to these reasons, this betting line is always quite popular amongst most football bettors, particularly those who want to keep their goals lines reasonably low.

How to Research Over 1.5 Goals Predictions

The biggest mistake made at this line is betting a huge club and claiming that the game would end with goals. This does not have to be the case, due to the fact that a top team can perform quite defensively because of a highly significant fixture, or for other reasons. What we always analyze first is the statistical numbers, and not the name of the club.

If you want to do your own research, there are a few basic checks to perform:

  1. Recent result checks. The last five to ten games for each team must be analysed. If most of their last games contained two or more goals, this gives information that is valuable when predicting games. It's not gospel that there will be goals, it's just a starter for the information.
  2. Home and Away performance. This point separates the wheat from the chaff in many cases, as many sides perform completely differently at home compared to how they perform away from home. Some sides will routinely pound their own fans with goals yet away from home struggle to make many clear chances. We show home stats and away stats separated in fixtures so it's easy to make up your mind.
  3. Goals Scored and Conceded stats. It's not enough to only check how many goals a team scores. You must also check how many it concedes. A team which scores two and concedes a goal or two is often a better bet than a team which beats others 1-0 consistently.
  4. Recent Head-to-Head Fixture Stats. Head to head form can be interesting, but we never allow it on its own to rule out a bet. A game that happened three years ago, for example, might well be useless, as the team of today will have changed a manager, its players and its tactics since that date.
  5. Team News. This is one area that very many prediction pages simply cannot help with. If, for example, you bet on the top striker scoring first but his team is a bit weak without him, you lose unless you have seen all the squad news.
