An Over 1.5 goals bet wins when a match produces at least two goals in total. You do not need one team to win and both teams do not need to score. A 2-0, 3-0, 1-1 or 2-1 result all land the bet; 0-0, 1-0 and 0-1 do not.

What Does Over 1.5 Goals Mean?

Over 1.5 is a lower goals line that asks for two or more goals across 90 minutes. It is popular with bettors who want a goals market without requiring a high-scoring match. Even so, it should be judged on the individual fixture rather than the reputation of a club or league.

A strong team is not automatically an Over 1.5 selection. Important fixtures, tactical approaches, injuries and opponent quality can all affect how a game is played. We look at the numbers around the match first—not just the names involved.

  5. Team news: Injuries, suspensions and likely line-ups can materially change a fixture's goal potential.

Over 1.5 Goals FAQ

Which scores win an Over 1.5 goals bet?

Any result with two or more total goals wins, including 1-1, 2-0, 2-1 and 3-0. Results of 0-0, 1-0 and 0-1 lose.

Which leagues are best for Over 1.5 goals?

Some leagues can show stronger historical scoring trends, but there is no single league that is reliable for every fixture. Check current form, team news and match-specific data before deciding.

Should I choose Over 1.5 or Over 2.5 goals?

Over 1.5 generally needs fewer goals to win than Over 2.5, so its odds are usually lower. The right choice depends on the fixture, available price and your own risk tolerance.

Can I use Over 1.5 goals in an accumulator?

You can, but every added leg increases the chance that the bet fails. We recommend assessing each fixture on its own merits rather than adding matches only to increase the potential return.
