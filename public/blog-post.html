What is the Over 2.5 Goals Market?

The over 2.5 goals bet wins if three or more goals are scored in a match, regardless of which team scores or who wins. It is a simple, market-agnostic bet that depends entirely on total goals scored.

In the 2024/25 Premier League season, approximately 62% of all matches ended with three or more goals — making it statistically one of the more achievable bets in top-flight European football.
