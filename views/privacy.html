Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 9, 2026

Rooted Predictions ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our website at www.rootedpredictions.com.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of personal data:

  • Account data: Name, email address, country, and hashed password when you register.
  • Usage data: Pages visited, predictions viewed, time on site, and browser/device information collected via server logs and analytics.
  • Bet history: Stake, odds, result, and notes you voluntarily enter into the bet tracking tool.
  • Payment data: Billing information is processed by Stripe or Paystack. We do not store card numbers on our servers.
  • Communications: Content of messages sent via our contact form.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain your account and subscription.
  • To send transactional emails (registration confirmation, password reset, VIP confirmation).
  • To process payments via Stripe and Paystack.
  • To improve our prediction algorithm and site performance.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties. We do not use your data for advertising profiling.

3. Cookies

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and remember your dark/light mode preference. We also use Google Analytics cookies to understand site usage. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, but some functionality may be affected.

4. Data Sharing

We share your data only with the following trusted third parties:

  • Stripe — Payment processing (global subscriptions)
  • Paystack — Payment processing (African users)
  • Google Analytics — Anonymised usage statistics
  • DigitalOcean — Hosting infrastructure

5. Data Retention

We retain your account data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data is removed within 30 days, except where we are required to retain it for legal or financial compliance purposes.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are based in the European Economic Area or the UK, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access your personal data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

To exercise any of these rights, email us at privacy@rootedpredictions.com.

7. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Passwords are hashed with bcrypt and never stored in plain text. Payment data is handled entirely by PCI-compliant processors (Stripe, Paystack) and never stored on our servers.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically. Material changes will be notified by email to registered users. Continued use of Rooted Predictions after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, contact us at privacy@rootedpredictions.com or via our contact page.
